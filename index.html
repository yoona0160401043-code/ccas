How to play 🚇

  • Make 4 teams. Click a team name to change it. 分4組，可按隊名修改。
  • Race from Central to Chai Wan. The first team to reach Chai Wan wins! 由中環出發，最先到柴灣的隊伍勝出。
  • Give points with the buttons (−1, +1, +2, +3). The train moves by that many stations. 按分數鍵，列車就移動相應站數。
  • 🟡 Express stations move you 1 more. 🔴 Signal fault sends you back 1. 黃色站加一站，紅色站退一站。
  • 📚 All words and sentences come from today's lesson. 所有題目都來自今課內容。
  • ⚡ Flash: a word flashes for a second. Every team writes it. Correct spelling +1.
  • 🧊 Iceberg: the letters are frozen and melt a few at a time. Put up your hand to guess the word! The earlier you guess, the more points. Wrong guess = your team waits one turn.
  • ✍️ Speed Write: remember the sentence and write it. Check spelling, capital letters and the full stop!
